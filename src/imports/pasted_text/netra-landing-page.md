============================================================
1. PROJECT
============================================================

Project name:

NETRA

Full name:

National Entity & Threat Relationship Analytics

Descriptor:

AI-Powered Criminal Network Intelligence Platform

Event:

Smart India Hackathon 2026

The project addresses the following problem:

Modern criminal activities are increasingly organized and interconnected. Criminal networks can involve associates, intermediaries, financial channels, communication links, locations, vehicles, organizations and events.

Law-enforcement agencies may collect information from:

• FIRs and police reports
• Call Detail Records (CDRs)
• Financial transaction records
• Surveillance reports
• Social media intelligence
• Criminal history databases
• Intelligence agency reports
• Vehicle-related records
• Case records
• Incident and event information

However, this information is often fragmented, unstructured and distributed across different systems.

Investigators may therefore struggle to identify hidden relationships, recurring patterns and important connections.

NETRA is proposed as an AI-powered investigation-support platform that can process authorized structured and unstructured information, extract entities, discover relationships, construct knowledge graphs, identify important network structures, detect suspicious patterns and present explainable analytical insights.

The landing page must communicate this concept clearly within the first few seconds.
